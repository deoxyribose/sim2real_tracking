DETR-Slot experiments — flagellum tracking

Three iterations on the object-centric refactor of sim2real_tracking. All models are sim-trained (foreground rendered onto real background patches), evaluated on 59 real painted flagellum annotations.

Milestones

v1 — DETR-Slot baseline

Slot-attention encoder → distributional heads (∅/pipette/cell/flagellum + mean+σ). No cell class.

Coverage k=2 = 12.5%, k=3 = 51.0%, k=5 = 99.0%. 22 min training on RTX 5090.

v2 — Cell class + boundary attachment + CP-SAT verifier

Sim scenes have cells; flagella attach on cell membrane. Model adds CellHead. CP-SAT verifier picks non-overlapping subset with attach-on-cell IP constraint.

Coverage k=2 = 50.0%, k=3 = 77.9%, k=5 = 97.1%. Verifier F1 = 0.45. Best for downstream that wants one selected curve per flagellum.

v3 — Structural cell-anchored flagellum head

Attachment reparameterized as (cell_slot, angle, radius). Attach-on-cell guaranteed by architecture, not statistics. Compares all three approaches side-by-side.

Coverage k=2 = 26.9%, k=3 = 65.4-87.5%, k=5 = 100.0%. Best broad coverage — samples always land somewhere near GT.

Framework

The 2026-08-28 refactor: replace reconstruction-loss object-centric video models with DETR-with-slot-attention emitting distributional latents. Sample many curves per slot, score them, pick a subset via IP.

Training data: sim foregrounds composited on real BG patches harvested from your videos.

Verifier: adapted from ~/discrete_linking_opt (Meyer et al.) — OR-Tools CP-SAT with reconstruction/energy scoring, at-most-one pixel constraints, attach-on-cell constraint, cardinality caps.
